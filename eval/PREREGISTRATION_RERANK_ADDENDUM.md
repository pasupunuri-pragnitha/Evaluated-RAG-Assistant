Written before any reranker run. Refusal threshold rule: the reranker relevance score is the sigmoid of the cross-encoder logit of the best of the 20 reranked candidates for a query. The threshold is the lowest such top score among v1 answerable questions (so v1 false refusals = 0 by construction). It is computed once on v1 and applied unchanged to v2. It is not recomputed on v2. The cosine gate (MIN_SCORE 0.60 on top_vector_score) is also reported.
