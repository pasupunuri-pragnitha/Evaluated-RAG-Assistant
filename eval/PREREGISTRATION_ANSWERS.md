Written before any answer-evaluation run. Pipeline: the default /ask configuration (RETRIEVER=hybrid-bm25, USE_RERANKER=false, k=5, MIN_SCORE=0.60 on top_vector_score, local llama31-rag, temperature 0, current prompt). No prompt, threshold or retriever change after seeing results; any change is a new version reported separately and the original numbers stay. Sets: v1 (eval/questions.jsonl, development) and v2 (eval/questions_v2.jsonl, held-out). One run per set, plus a 5-question determinism re-run. Metrics (counts and percentages): (A) retrievable-answerable = answerable questions with an expected-page chunk in the top 5; false-refusal rate = refused / retrievable-answerable. (B) page-grounded rate = answered retrievable-answerable questions whose cited sources include an expected page / answered retrievable-answerable. (C) unanswerable questions: refusal rate (gate or LLM) and the list of those answered. (D) share of answers with any warning, and mean and max total latency. (E) Manual review of up to 15 answered questions per set, scored by me as correct, partly correct, or incorrect. No LLM judges any answer.
